Frequently asked questions

Workflow automation, explained

What is workflow automation?

Workflow automation uses software to move information between systems and carry out repeatable steps—such as creating a task, updating a record, requesting approval or sending a notification—based on defined rules.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Usually not. Where supported, workflows can connect tools you already use. We assess your current systems and integrations before recommending a design.

Can AI make decisions automatically?

AI can help classify, summarize and extract information. For sensitive or consequential actions, the workflow can require a human to review and approve the result.

Can you build a workflow for our specific industry?

Yes. We begin by mapping the task, identifying systems and data involved, and agreeing on the workflow, permissions, exception handling and success measures.
