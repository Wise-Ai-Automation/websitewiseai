Frequently asked questions

Designed for trustworthy knowledge access

Will the chatbot make up policy answers?

A production system should be designed to retrieve relevant approved documents, cite the supporting passages, acknowledge when evidence is missing and escalate uncertain questions. Testing and ongoing monitoring are essential; no AI system should be treated as infallible.

Can each organization keep its documents separate?

Yes, the intended product design is multi-tenant: each customer’s documents, users, access rules and activity should be separated and tested. The actual security controls depend on the implemented platform and hosting configuration.

Can we use it in a nursing home?

It can be designed as a policy-reference and training aid using approved documents. It should not replace clinical judgment, required training, emergency processes, or the organization’s established escalation pathway.

Does this automatically make our organization PHIPA compliant?

No. Compliance depends on the data, roles, contracts, hosting, access controls, retention, audit practices and actual operating model. Healthcare deployments require a privacy and security assessment and appropriate legal review before launch.

Can it connect to our existing systems?

Potential integrations can include identity providers, intranets, document repositories, learning systems and ticketing tools. Feasibility and security need to be reviewed for each system.
