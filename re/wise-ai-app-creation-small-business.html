Frequently asked questions

Understand the basics before investing in an app.

Do I need separate iPhone and Android apps?

Not always. A responsive web app or progressive web app may cover the first version. Native iOS and Android apps can be considered when device features, app-store distribution or user needs justify the extra cost.

Can the app connect to my existing software?

Often, yes, if the vendor offers a suitable API or supported integration. Feasibility, access permissions, subscription fees and ongoing maintenance are reviewed during discovery.

Can you add AI?

AI can support document extraction, knowledge search, summaries and task classification. Important actions should have validation, permissions and human approval where appropriate.

Who owns the app and its data?

Ownership, source-code access, hosting, data export, third-party licences and maintenance responsibilities should be defined in the project agreement before development begins.

Can we start with a small budget?

Yes. A focused prototype or minimum viable product can test one core workflow before investing in advanced features or multiple platforms.
