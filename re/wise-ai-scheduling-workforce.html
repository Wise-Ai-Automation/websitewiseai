Frequently asked questions

Common questions about scheduling and workforce automation.

Can customers book and reschedule appointments online?

Yes. A production setup can show available times, confirm bookings, handle rescheduling and cancellations, and send notifications when connected to a calendar and messaging service.

Can the system manage staff schedules and open shifts?

Yes. It can support roster planning, shift publication, availability collection and assignment workflows. Eligibility rules and manager approval should be configured for each organization.

How does a staff call-out work?

A call-out can flag the affected shift, notify the authorized scheduler, identify eligible coverage options and track the outcome. It should not automatically assign staff without the required checks and approvals.

Can it integrate with our existing software?

Potentially, depending on the software's API, webhook and access capabilities. Integrations should be confirmed during discovery before promising a specific connection.

Is this page a live scheduling system?

No. This page demonstrates the proposed product and sample workflows. Live bookings, employee notifications, authentication, calendar synchronization and staffing rules require a connected backend and tested integrations.
