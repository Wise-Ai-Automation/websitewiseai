Trust should be part of the product.

We plan for privacy and security from the beginning—not as an afterthought. Actual legal obligations depend on your business, information and provinces served.

✓
Clear collection notices

Explain what information is collected, why it is needed and how to contact your business.

✓
Limited access and retention

Transparent AI interactions
