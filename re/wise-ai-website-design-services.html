Frequently asked questions

What to know before starting your website project.

Can you build a website for my restaurant?

Yes. A restaurant website can include a digital menu, gallery, hours, location, reservation links, online-ordering integration and catering inquiry forms.

Can customers book appointments on my website?

Yes. We can integrate a suitable booking platform or scope a custom booking workflow. Availability, reminders and payments depend on the selected provider and integration.

Do you provide hosting and domain registration?

These can be included in a managed package or arranged separately. The final quote will specify ownership, renewal costs and support responsibilities.

Can you connect the website to my CRM or other tools?

Yes, where suitable integrations or APIs are available. Each integration is reviewed for feasibility, cost, access and ongoing vendor fees.

Will my website work on phones?

Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop are part of the proposed website scope.
